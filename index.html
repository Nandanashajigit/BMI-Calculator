Your history

BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis. It cannot tell muscle from fat, and it reads differently across ages, builds and ethnic groups. Body fat and calorie figures are estimates. For children and teens (2–17), BMI must be read against age- and sex-specific growth percentiles, so this page shows the number for reference only. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making health decisions.
